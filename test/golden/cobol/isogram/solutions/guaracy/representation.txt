IDENTIFICATION DIVISION .
  PROGRAM-ID . ISOGRAM .
  ENVIRONMENT DIVISION .
  DATA DIVISION .
  WORKING-STORAGE SECTION .
  01 WS-PHRASE PIC X ( 60 ).
  01 WS-RESULT PIC 99 .
  01 WS-CHARS PIC X ( 26 ) VALUE 'ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ' .
  01 REDEFINES WS-CHARS .
    05 WS-CHAR PIC X OCCURS 26 TIMES .
  01 WS-FLAG PIC 9 VALUE ZEROS .
    88 HAS-MANY VALUE 1 .
  01 WS-COUNT PIC 9 ( 3 ).
  01 WS-IX PIC 99 .
    PROCEDURE DIVISION .
    ISOGRAM .
      MOVE FUNCTION UPPER-CASE ( WS-PHRASE ) TO WS-PHRASE
      MOVE 1 TO WS-RESULT
      PERFORM VARYING WS-IX FROM 1 BY 1 UNTIL WS-IX > 26
        MOVE 0 TO WS-COUNT
        INSPECT WS-PHRASE TALLYING WS-COUNT FOR ALL WS-CHAR ( WS-IX )
        IF WS-COUNT > 1
          MOVE 1 TO WS-FLAG
      END-PERFORM .
      IF HAS-MANY
        MOVE 0 TO WS-RESULT
      GOBACK .
