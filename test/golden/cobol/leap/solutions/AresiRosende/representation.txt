IDENTIFICATION DIVISION .
  PROGRAM-ID . LEAP .
  ENVIRONMENT DIVISION .
  DATA DIVISION .
  WORKING-STORAGE SECTION .
  01 WS-YEAR PIC 9 ( 4 ).
  01 WS-RESULT PIC 9 ( 4 ).
  01 R-100 PIC 9 ( 3 ).
  01 R-400 PIC 9 ( 3 ).
  01 R-4 PIC 9 ( 1 ).
  01 AUX PIC 9 ( 3 ).
  PROCEDURE DIVISION .
  LEAP .
    DIVIDE 4 INTO WS-YEAR GIVING AUX REMAINDER R-4 .
    DIVIDE 400 INTO WS-YEAR GIVING AUX REMAINDER R-400 .
    DIVIDE 100 INTO WS-YEAR GIVING AUX REMAINDER R-100 .
    IF ( R-400 = 0 ) OR ( R-4 = 0 AND NOT R-100 = 0 ) THEN
      MOVE 1 TO WS-RESULT
    ELSE
      MOVE 0 TO WS-RESULT
    END-IF
    CONTINUE .
  LEAP-EXIT .
    EXIT .
