standardize ( Word , Lower , Sorted ):-
string_lower ( Word , Lower ),
atom_chars ( Lower , Chars ),
msort ( Chars , Sorted ).
is_anagram ( A , B ):-
standardize ( A , LowerA , Sorted ),
standardize ( B , LowerB , Sorted ),
\ + LowerA == LowerB .
anagram ( Word , Candidates , Anagrams ):-
include ( is_anagram ( Word ), Candidates , Anagrams ).
