sum_of_multiples ( Factors , Limit , Sum ):-
convlist ({ Limit } / [ F , M ] >> multiples ( F , Limit , M ), Factors , AllMultiples ),
foldl ( union , AllMultiples , [], UniqueMultiples ),
sum_list ( UniqueMultiples , Sum ).
multiples ( Base , Limit , Multiples ):-
multiples ( Base , Limit , Multiples , Base , []).
multiples ( _Base , Limit , Multiples , Current , MultiplesSoFar ):-
Current >= Limit ,
Multiples = MultiplesSoFar ,
!.
multiples ( Base , Limit , Multiples , Current , MultiplesSoFar ):-
Current < Limit ,
Next is Current + Base ,
multiples ( Base , Limit , Multiples , Next , [ Current | MultiplesSoFar ]).
