s:trim

dup question?
swap yelling?
2dup and \ yelled-question?
if 2drop "\"Calm" down, I know what I'm doing!" ;; then \ a yelled question
if drop "\"Whoa," chill out!" ;; then \ yelling
if "\"Sure.\"" ;; then \ question
