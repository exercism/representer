CLASS zcl_prime_factors DEFINITION
PUBLIC
FINAL
CREATE PUBLIC .
PUBLIC SECTION .
TYPES integertab TYPE STANDARD TABLE OF i WITH EMPTY KEY .
METHODS factors
IMPORTING
input TYPE int8
RETURNING
VALUE ( result ) TYPE integertab .
PROTECTED SECTION .
PRIVATE SECTION .
ENDCLASS .
CLASS zcl_prime_factors IMPLEMENTATION .
METHOD factors .
DATA ( temp_input ) = input .
DATA ( divisor ) = 2 .
WHILE divisor < temp_input .
WHILE temp_input MOD divisor = 0 .
temp_input = temp_input / divisor .
APPEND divisor TO result .
ENDWHILE .
divisor = divisor + 1 .
ENDWHILE .
IF temp_input >= 2 .
APPEND temp_input TO result .
ENDIF .
ENDMETHOD .
ENDCLASS .
